Power Stability: USB vs MT3608 Boost

The board behaves differently on USB and on a battery + MT3608 because the two sources have very different output impedance.

USB-C from PC5 V battery → MT3608
SourceRegulated, 1–3 A availableSwitching boost, limited output caps
WiringShort, thickOften long/thin dupont or breadboard
Transient responseStiff; bulk caps absorb Wi-Fi burstsSags on a 300–500 mA TX pulse
ResultStable Wi-Fi + I²CBrownout resets, Wi-Fi drops, MPU NACKs

The ESP32 radio draws short 300–500 mA bursts at 3.3 V every beacon/TX window. The DevKit's AMS1117 needs ~1.1–1.3 V of headroom, so a 5.0 V boost set point is already marginal; if the MT3608 output dips to ~4.5 V the 3.3 V rail falls, the brownout detector trips, and the MPU6050 starts returning I²C NACKs. That is why the same firmware and the same open ESP32 AP look stable on USB and “sometimes connected” on battery.

Fix checklist

  1. Set the MT3608 to 5.3–5.5 V, measured at the ESP32 5V/VIN pin while

    2. the board is connected and transmitting — not open-circuit. Stay below 6 V.

  2. Add bulk capacitance at the board: 470–1000 µF low-ESR electrolytic plus

    3. 100 nF ceramic across 5V/GND, and 100 µF + 100 nF across 3V3/GND if the 3.3 V pin is accessible.

  3. Add 22–47 µF at the MT3608 input and output if the module lacks them.
  4. Use short, thick power wires (18–20 AWG), soldered or screw terminals.

    5. Do not run the boost output through a breadboard.

  5. Battery must supply ≥1 A continuous and ≥2 A peaks. Small protected

    6. 5 V packs and 9 V-style batteries sag or trip their protection.

  6. Use one power path at a time. Do not connect USB and the boost to 5V

    7. together; use a Schottky (SS34) or ideal-diode OR-ing if both are needed.

  7. Keep the boost inductor/switch node away from the GPS antenna and I²C

    8. wires. Twist SDA/SCL with a ground return and keep them under ~20 cm.

  8. Measure with a scope, not a multimeter. A multimeter averages away the

    9. millisecond dips. Target: 5 V rail stays above 4.8 V, 3.3 V above 3.1 V during connect/TX.

Firmware mitigations (already applied)

ChangeEffect
Wi-Fi TX power set to 15 dBmCuts the dominant current peak by roughly a third
sys.brown / boot banner brownouts NCounts actual brownout resets in NVS
I²C bus recovery after 5 failed readsRe-inits the bus instead of staying in MPU FAIL

These reduce the symptom; they do not replace the bulk capacitors and a correctly set boost output.
